# What We Eat

A Singapore food-place finder where people choose where to eat together, and keep their own record of where they have been.

## Language

### Places

**Place**:
A restaurant, cafe or other food place listed in the app.
_Avoid_: Restaurant (when the place may be a cafe or food stall), venue, listing

### Deciding together

**Shortlist**:
The few places a person picks, before sending them to friends to vote on.
_Avoid_: Cart, list

**Poll**:
A shortlist sent to friends as a link that they vote on without signing in.
_Avoid_: Survey, ballot

### Personal record

**Target**:
A place a person has aimed at as the next one to go to.
_Avoid_: Saved, bookmark, favourite, marker, wishlist

**Conquered Spot**:
A person's own record of a place they have been to, with their rating and note. It is private unless they choose to share it.
_Avoid_: Journal, review, check-in, visit, foodprint

**Shared Conquered Spot**:
A Conquered Spot its owner has chosen to show to everyone, on that place's page.
_Avoid_: Public review, post

## Retired working names

"Marker" and "Journal" were working names for Target and Conquered Spot and were never released. "Saved" and "Review" are the names being replaced in the app.
